Family Guy is an American animated sitcom starring the Griffin Family consisting of Peter, Lois, Chris, Meg, Stewie, and Brian
  • Peter: THE family guy, is the dad, he is fat and a drunk. He is always in shenanigans.
  • Lois: Peter's wife and the mom, she keeps the house in order.
  • Meg: Formally known as Megatron, she is the teenage daughter of Peter and Lois, she is constantly harrassed by everyone around her.
  • Chris: He is the teenage son of Peter and Lois, he is very unintellegient.
  • Stewie: He is the evil baby of Peter and Lois, he is very smart and capability of making weapons and a time machine.
  • Brian: Brian is the dog of the household, he likes to write books and pretends to be intelligent.
Family Guy's best recieved episode is Season 8 Episode 1 "Road to the Multiverse" Starring Stewie and Brian as they travel the multiverse. The episode starts off with Stewie showing off his genetically mutated pig to Brian stating that he got it from the multiverse. Stewie then takes Brian throughout the multiverse until they eventually break the remote, and have to figure out how to fix the remote within the multiverse.
